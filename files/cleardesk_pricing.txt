ClearDesk Pricing Plans — Complete Reference
=============================================
Last updated: March 2025 | Version: 4.2

ClearDesk offers three plans: Starter, Pro, and Enterprise. All plans are billed per seat (licensed member). Guest users do not count as seats. Prices listed are in USD.

---

STARTER PLAN
------------
Price: Free (up to 5 members) or USD 5 per member per month (up to 20 members)
Billing: Monthly only
Minimum seats: 1
Maximum seats: 20

Starter is designed for small teams or individuals getting started with project management. It includes core task and project functionality with reasonable limits.

Included in Starter:
- Up to 10 active projects
- Unlimited tasks and subtasks (within active projects)
- List view, Board view, Calendar view (Timeline view not included)
- Task priorities, statuses, due dates, assignees, and followers
- File attachments: up to 25 MB per file
- Workspace storage: 5 GB total
- Basic automations: up to 20 active automation rules
- In-app and email notifications
- iOS and Android mobile apps
- Recurring tasks
- CSV import and export
- Help Centre access and community forums
- Email support (response within 48 hours on business days)

Not included in Starter:
- Timeline (Gantt) view
- Task dependencies
- Portfolios
- Time tracking
- Custom fields
- Guest users
- Slack, GitHub, and webhook integrations
- Google Drive and Dropbox integration
- SAML SSO
- Priority support
- Workload and burndown reports
- Audit logs

---

PRO PLAN
---------
Price: USD 12 per member per month (billed annually) or USD 15 per member per month (billed monthly)
Annual billing discount: 20% off the monthly rate
Minimum seats: 1
Maximum seats: 500

Pro is designed for growing teams that need advanced scheduling, integrations, automation, and reporting. It is the most popular plan for mid-sized businesses.

Included in Pro (everything in Starter, plus):
- Up to 100 active projects
- Timeline (Gantt) view with dependencies and milestones
- Task dependencies
- Time tracking (per task, with export)
- Custom fields: up to 50 per workspace (text, number, date, dropdown, checkbox, URL)
- Guest users: up to 5 guests per licensed seat (guests are free)
- File attachments: up to 100 MB per file
- Workspace storage: 100 GB total
- Advanced automations: up to 200 active automation rules
- Portfolios: unlimited portfolios with workload view
- Workload and burndown reports
- Slack integration (per-project notification configuration)
- Google Drive integration (attach and preview Google Docs/Sheets/Slides)
- Dropbox and OneDrive integration
- GitHub integration (PR and commit linking)
- Zapier and Make (Integromat) integration
- Webhooks: up to 10 endpoints per workspace
- Mandatory two-factor authentication (workspace-wide enforcement)
- Priority email support: response within 24 hours on business days
- In-app chat support: Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM IST
- 90-day audit log retention
- Data export: full workspace export on demand (JSON + CSV)

Not included in Pro:
- SAML SSO / single sign-on
- Jira two-way sync
- Custom reports
- Unlimited guests
- Dedicated Customer Success Manager
- 99.9% uptime SLA
- Data residency options
- Audit log retention beyond 90 days
- Advanced security controls (IP allowlisting, session management)

---

ENTERPRISE PLAN
----------------
Price: Custom (contact sales@cleardesk.io for a quote)
Billing: Annual only (multi-year discounts available)
Minimum seats: 20
Maximum seats: Unlimited

Enterprise is designed for large organisations with complex security, compliance, administration, and integration requirements. Pricing is based on seat count, contract length, and required add-ons.

Included in Enterprise (everything in Pro, plus):
- Unlimited active projects
- Unlimited guests (no per-seat cap)
- File attachments: up to 500 MB per file
- Workspace storage: Unlimited
- Unlimited automation rules
- Custom reports: build, save, and share reports using any task field or custom field
- Jira two-way sync (bi-directional task and status mapping)
- SAML 2.0 SSO with any identity provider (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, OneLogin, PingFederate, ADFS)
- SCIM provisioning: automatically provision and deprovision user accounts from your identity provider
- Advanced security: IP allowlisting, session timeout controls, login attempt monitoring
- Data residency: choose between US, EU, or APAC data hosting regions (subject to availability)
- Audit logs: 1-year retention, full activity log downloadable as CSV
- 99.9% uptime SLA (monthly, contractually guaranteed)
- Dedicated Customer Success Manager from day one
- Priority support: 4-hour response time, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
- Quarterly business reviews with your CSM
- Custom onboarding and training sessions
- Sandbox environment: separate non-production workspace for testing
- Volume licensing and multi-workspace management for enterprise accounts with multiple teams

ADD-ONS (available on Pro and Enterprise)
------------------------------------------
The following add-ons can be purchased separately and billed monthly:

Additional Storage: USD 10 per month per 100 GB additional storage. Available on Pro plan only (Enterprise has unlimited storage).
Advanced Analytics Pack: USD 8 per member per month. Adds advanced burndown/burnup charts, custom dashboards, and the ability to export reports via API. Available on Pro and Enterprise.
Enhanced Security Pack: USD 6 per member per month on Pro. Adds IP allowlisting, enhanced session management, and extended audit log retention (1 year). On Enterprise, this is included by default.

BILLING AND PAYMENT
---------------------
Accepted payment methods:
- Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) — all plans
- ACH bank transfer (US accounts only) — Pro and Enterprise
- Wire transfer — Enterprise only (minimum USD 5,000 per invoice)
- Purchase orders — Enterprise only

Billing cycle options:
- Monthly: billed on the same date each month. Cancel anytime, effective at end of current period.
- Annual: billed once per year, 20% discount vs monthly rate. Refunds are not provided for mid-year cancellations, but you may downgrade at renewal.

Invoices: Automatically generated and emailed to the billing contact at each charge. Available for download from Settings > Billing > Invoice History.

Taxes: ClearDesk collects applicable taxes (GST, VAT, sales tax) based on the billing address provided. Indian customers are charged 18% GST. EU customers with a valid VAT ID are billed without VAT (reverse charge mechanism applies).

Currency: All charges are in USD. If your card is in another currency, your bank or card issuer handles conversion and may charge a foreign transaction fee. INR pricing is available for Indian annual contracts — contact sales for a local-currency quote.

Seat changes mid-cycle:
- Adding seats: charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing period, then at the new rate from the next cycle.
- Removing seats: effective at the start of the next billing period. The reduced count must still meet the plan minimum.

Free Trial: New workspaces receive a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan, no credit card required. At the end of the trial, the workspace automatically moves to the Starter (free) plan unless a Pro or Enterprise subscription is purchased.

DATA SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE
------------------------------
ClearDesk infrastructure is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS). Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+).

Certifications and compliance:
- SOC 2 Type II certified (annual audit)
- ISO 27001 certified
- GDPR compliant (Data Processing Agreement available on request for Pro and Enterprise customers)
- HIPAA: ClearDesk is not HIPAA certified. Customers handling Protected Health Information (PHI) should not store PHI in ClearDesk without a signed Business Associate Agreement. Contact sales@cleardesk.io to discuss options.

Backups: Automated daily backups retained for 30 days. Point-in-time recovery is available on Enterprise.

Data deletion: Upon account cancellation, data is permanently deleted after the 90-day retention window. Enterprise customers can request immediate deletion upon contract termination.

Sub-processors: A full list of ClearDesk sub-processors (including AWS, Stripe, SendGrid, and Intercom) is available at cleardesk.io/legal/sub-processors.
